Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data APPJS Technologies Private Limited ("APPJS", "we", "us") collects through this website and the apps we operate, why we collect it, and the choices you have. It applies to Restyle AI (the website at restyle.appjs.tech and the Restyle AI Android app) and to any other app of ours that links to this page.

In short: we collect what we need to sign you in, turn your photos into styled images and keep track of your credits. Your photos are sent to the AI service that produces the style you pick. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising, and you can delete your account at any time from the app.

1. Who we are

APPJS Technologies Private Limited is a company incorporated in India (CIN U62011DL2023PTC423950), based in Delhi. We are the data fiduciary (controller) for the personal data described here. You can reach us at contactappdojs@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

Account information

  • If you sign in with your phone number: your mobile number. We send a one-time code to it by SMS.
  • If you sign in with Google: your name, email address, profile picture and Google account identifier, as shared by Google when you choose to sign in.
  • A name you choose to set in your profile, if any.

Photos and creations

  • Photos you upload to be restyled, and the images we generate from them.
  • The style (template) and options you chose for each creation.

Credits and purchases

  • Your credit balance and a history of credits added and spent.
  • If you buy a plan or credits, the plan, amount and payment status. Card, UPI and bank details are handled by our payment partner; we do not receive or store them.

Technical information

  • A random identifier for your device or browser, your IP address, and the times you sign in and use the app. We use these to keep you signed in, to enforce usage limits and to prevent abuse.
  • Server logs of requests (for example errors and response times), kept for operating and securing the service.

We do not use advertising or analytics trackers in Restyle AI, and this website sets no cookies. The Restyle AI website stores your sign-in session in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in.

3. How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To generate the images you ask for and show them in "My creations".
  • To keep your credit balance, process purchases and give signup or plan credits.
  • To limit misuse (for example rate limits on sign-in codes and image generation) and keep the service running.
  • To answer your messages and requests.
  • To meet our legal obligations, such as tax and accounting records.

We do not sell your personal data, use it for advertising, or use your photos to train AI models.

4. Google user data

When you use "Sign in with Google", we receive only your basic profile (name, email address, profile picture) and a Google account identifier. We use them only to create your account and show who is signed in. We do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data, and we do not share Google user data with anyone except as needed to run the service as described in this policy. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Who we share it with

We share personal data only with service providers that help us run the app, and only what each one needs:

  • AI image services (such as Google Gemini, OpenAI, fal.ai and OpenRouter): your uploaded photo and the style instructions, to produce your image. Which service is used depends on the style you pick.
  • SMS providers (such as MSG91, or Google Firebase where used): your mobile number, to send your sign-in code.
  • Google, when you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Payment partners, when you make a purchase.
  • Cloud hosting providers that run our servers and store data.

Some of these providers process data outside India. We may also disclose data if the law requires it, or to protect our users, our rights or the public.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account information is kept while your account is open. When you delete your account, we remove your phone number, email address, name, profile picture and Google identifier and end all your sessions.
  • Your photos and creations are kept so you can see and download them. You can ask us to delete them at any time by writing to us; we will do so within 30 days.
  • Records of credits and purchases are kept, without your contact details, for as long as tax and accounting laws require.
  • Sign-in codes expire after a few minutes; technical logs are kept only as long as needed for security and operations.

7. Your rights and choices

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable law, you can ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, and nominate someone to exercise your rights. You can delete your account yourself in the app (Profile → Delete account), or write to us at the address below. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Sign-in codes are stored only in hashed form, and access to our systems is limited to people who need it. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you and the authorities of a breach as the law requires.

9. Children

Our apps are not meant for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the date at the top, and tell you in the app if a change is significant.

11. Contact and grievances

For questions, requests or complaints about your personal data, write to our Grievance Officer at contactappdojs@gmail.com.
